What it does to your spreadsheet

The add-on writes a tab named Comment Export into the spreadsheet you run it in. If a tab with that name already exists, its contents are cleared and replaced. Do not keep anything you care about in a tab with that name. It changes nothing else in your file, and it never modifies, resolves or deletes your actual comments.

Limits worth knowing

  • Google caps the export it relies on at 10MB, so a very large workbook may not run. The add-on tells you when that happens rather than failing silently.
  • It reads comments. Notes are a separate Sheets feature and do not appear.
  • Access is granted one file at a time. That is deliberate, and it is why the add-on never needs permission to your whole Drive.
