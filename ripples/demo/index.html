What did that thing everyone remembers change?

Pick a moment. Each ring is time since it happened. Ripples appear on the day they began, and every ripple starts after the one before it.

Measured: moved in the data, beyond normal, after the step before Timed: rose in the right order Reported: a credible source makes the link Plausible: it happened; the link is unproven Busted: wrong order, contradicted or no evidence

Attention first

Daily reading and search data show what people went looking for, and exactly when they started.

Then behaviour

Borrowing, buying, signing up: did attention turn into doing? Where it didn't, the map says so.

Then the world

Charts, sales, laws. Public records, dated and sourced, placed where they fall in time.

Order, not a web

A step that began before the step before it is dropped. A ripple is cause and effect in sequence, not a cloud of related topics.

An early prototype. Attention is measured from daily Wikipedia views; library borrowing from Seattle Public Library open data; records are linked to their sources. Timing shows order, not proof of cause. The full maps, with every test and every miss: ripple maps · discoveries.
