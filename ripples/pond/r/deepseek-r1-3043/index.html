How to read the pond

  • The stone is the event. Bigger stone, bigger shock.
  • Rings are time after it, a day to three months.
  • Lily pads are things that moved. Solid means Measured; half-filled means well supported but not yet Measured.
  • Wave height at a pad is how big the move was.
  • A dotted float is a series still waiting for its verdict.
  • Reeds absorb the wave: checked, stayed flat.
  • A cairn on the far shore marks where events like this one usually reach: a rule about many, not evidence about this one.
  • A boulder is a shock we filtered out; ripples bend around it.
  • A second stone nearby means overlapping rings we cannot cleanly separate.
