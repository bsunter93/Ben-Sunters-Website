Methods

How Ripple Map knows. Eight steps, then a word we are allowed to use.

The engine decides what may be said; the story layer only decides what is shown first, and can never upgrade a tier. Every number on a page traces to a sequence number in a hash-chained ledger. Consistent with, never proof of cause.

The eight steps

  1. Find the candidate

    A shock happens (a storm, a release, a policy date). A mechanism library, Wikidata links and family maps propose which series it could reach. This stage is permissive on purpose: discovery is allowed to be messy.

  2. Freeze the hypothesis

    Before anyone looks at the outcome, the series, its window, the expected sign and a prior are written to the ledger. A rule chosen after looking is disclosed as exactly that.

  3. Measure the move

    The series is compared with its own season-matched normal: the same dates in every other year, never after the event. The move is a ratio to normal, shrunk toward zero.

  4. Test lookalikes

    The same test runs on hundreds of fake dates for the same series, on other series at the real date, and on random links. "About 1 in 412 fake dates look this strong" is that number.

  5. Compare independent sources

    Physical, attention and economic channels are counted separately. Where the shock is regional, the hit region is put against every region it missed, with in-space and in-time placebos and a synthetic control. An independent forecast model gets the last word on Measured.

  6. Replicate on similar past events

    "Seen after N of M similar events." The per-event flip-through is on every rule page, including the ones that did not move.

  7. Correct for multiple testing

    Benjamini–Hochberg across every frozen pair, plus a decoy false-alarm rate measured on links that cannot be real. A family of 25 is shown whole, never only the winner.

  8. Publish

    The gated tier, the frozen version and the ledger head go to the page, the share card and the OG image together. A retraction reaches every surface the same way. The full methods page has the maths.

The words we are allowed to use

Colour is never the only channel: each tier is also a shape and a word. A lower tier may never look brighter than a higher one.

MeasuredThe engine's top tier and the independent forecast check agree. Still consistent with, never proof of cause.
LikelyWell supported by the engine's test; the final look or the forecast check is still to come. A fluke is not ruled out.
WatchingRegistered, window still open. Too early to tell. "Similar events: a move by then about 1 in 5 times."
Stayed flatThe window closed without a detectable move. A dead end we disclose is a finding too.

Glossary

The pond is a data display that happens to look like a pond. Every element encodes a field by a stated rule.

  • RippleOne event and everything it was registered to reach. The stone is the shock; bigger stone, bigger shock.
  • RingsTime after the event: Day 1, Week 1, Month 1, Month 3. A thing's distance from the stone is its lag; its angle is its land.
  • StopA series the ripple reached. A lily pad on the water; its size and the wave at it are the size of the move.
  • BranchEvent → A and event → B, drawn as a fork. A chain (event → A → B) is drawn only when the engine's mediation check supports it.
  • EchoSeveral shores moving at once after one stone. An archetype: a story label, never evidence.
  • Dead endA series we said in advance might move, and did not. Reeds absorb the wave. "The ripple that died."
  • Common stopTwo stones, one stop: both moved after X. Drawn as a fork, never a chain, because a common cause is possible.
  • Deep rippleHow far did it travel? Domains crossed, days elapsed, order (a 2nd-order ripple went through another stop). A property of the world, not a score.
  • World ruleA pattern across many past events ("across 23 hurricanes…"). Drawn as a cairn on the far shore of an event's pond because it is not evidence about that event.
  • BoulderA common shock the engine filtered out (a holiday, an election week). Ripples visibly bend around it.
  • WindowThe dates, written down first, inside which a move counts. "Expected to resolve in 4 days" is the return loop.
  • Second stoneAnother event inside the window whose rings overlap. Shown so you can see what we could not cleanly separate.

Archetypes (story labels only)

Detour (the ripple reached a shore nobody was watching), Echo, Delay (nothing, then a late swell), Funnel (several stops on one shore), Bounce (a rise, then a reversal), Amplifier (small stone, big wave), Blind Spot (it moved where nobody was looking), Branch, Shared Stop, Dead end, Ghost (every expected stop stayed flat). The tag is chosen by the story layer from the evidence fields; the pond draws the same geometry whatever the tag says.

The kill log

every frozen pair, nulls included

Pairs that were tested and did not clear the bar, with why. A rule that survives is shown next to the ones that died.

    What we refuse to ship

    A tier word stronger than the gate returned, on any surface. A chain drawn where mediation wasn't supported. A window or donor pool chosen after looking, unless disclosed as exactly that. A family of 25 where only the winner is shown. Causal verbs. Beauty that makes Likely feel like Measured. Celebration around a disaster. Hitting a target by lowering a bar.
