Consistent with, never proof of cause. Each stop is a public series tested against its own weekday and seasonal normal, after the shock, with placebo dates, decoy starts and a published count of every path we tried.

Sources include Wikimedia pageviews (CC0), TSA, MTA, OpenFEMA, EIA, FRED, BLS, the Department of Labor, npm, PyPI, Hacker News, Stack Exchange, Indeed Hiring Lab and Polymarket; every stop lists its own sources and licences. This product uses the FEMA OpenFEMA API, but is not endorsed or certified by FEMA. Not affiliated with the Wikimedia Foundation.

Privacy: We keep no accounts and no reading history. If you tap Share or arrive from a shared link, we count it: a daily count per source, with a random browser ID hashed under a salt we delete each day. No IP address, no page history. Waitlist emails are stored only for that list and can't be read back through the site. Your theme, last visit and private hunches stay in your browser.
