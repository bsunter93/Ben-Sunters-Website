effect_chart_v2 (fixed prompt: the numbers for one study's effect chart, from the paper's full text)

Input: a JSON list of items {slug, headline, stone, claim, grade, effect, note, fulltext}. fulltext is the path to the
paper's text (converted from PDF or XML; line breaks and hyphenation may be broken), or of its abstract when no full text was reachable.

For each item, find the paper's own number for the finding the story states (the claim and headline), preferring the
headline estimate in the abstract, introduction or conclusion over a table cell. For a busted or disputed item, find
what the study measured (often close to zero), not the claim it tested.

Write one object per item to the output file (a JSON list, in input order), in ONE of these kinds:
1. "pct": a percentage change against what would have happened otherwise.
   {"slug", "kind": "pct", "base_label", "bars": [{"label", "pct"} or {"label", "pct_lo", "pct_hi"}], "unit", "quote"}
   pct is signed (+18 for 18% more, -2.7 for 2.7% fewer); a range is pct_lo/pct_hi. 1 to 3 bars, main finding first.
2. "levels": both levels are given (a rate, a share, a count with and without, or before and after).
   {"slug", "kind": "levels", "bars": [{"label", "v", "base": true}, {"label", "v"}], "unit", "quote"}
3. "groups": the effect itself in a natural unit (points, cases, days, dollars) for 1 to 3 groups.
   {"slug", "kind": "groups", "bars": [{"label", "v"} or {"label", "lo", "hi"}], "unit", "quote", "max": optional}
   Add "max": 100 when the unit is percentage points of a share.
4. "none": the paper gives no number for this finding that a reader could compare.
   {"slug", "kind": "none", "reason"}

Rules:
- quote is ONE contiguous passage from the fulltext, at most 60 words, copied as it appears; you may join a broken line
  with a single space and rejoin a word split by a hyphen at a line end. Every number in bars must appear as numerals in
  quote. Do not compute, convert, average or round, except changing the sign for "fewer", "lower", "less", "drop",
  "decline", "fall". A standard-deviation effect ("0.1 SD") is not a percent: use "groups" with unit "standard
  deviations", or "none".
- The number must be for the same outcome and population the claim names. If the paper's number is for something
  narrower or different, say so in unit (at most 14 words) or use "none".
- base_label 1 to 4 words; labels at most 5 words; plain American English; no em dashes.
- Read only the input file, this prompt and the fulltext files it names. Do not look anything up or open other files.
Then check with Python: as many entries as items, in order; for each quote, after collapsing all whitespace to single
spaces in both, the quote is a substring of the collapsed fulltext; every number in bars appears in quote. Fix any
failure. Final message: "done, N written, P pct, L levels, G groups, X none".
