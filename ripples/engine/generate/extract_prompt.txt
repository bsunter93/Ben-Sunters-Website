extract_v4 (raw-pair extraction for the discovery v4 pilot)

You read a batch of papers (title, year, venue, abstract). For each paper decide whether it yields raw pairs, and extract
them. Use only the text given. Do not search, do not open other files, do not use outside knowledge to add facts.

A raw pair is (stone, outcome) from one paper where all three hold:
1. STONE: the paper names a specific, datable cultural event in one of these classes:
   I1 sports results and big games (Super Bowl, World Cup, upsets, home-team wins and losses, star signings)
   I2 media arrival and rollout (TV, radio, cable, satellite, broadband, mobile coverage, a channel entering a market)
   I3 entertainment releases (films, video games, series, albums, books)
   I4 celebrity moments (deaths, scandals, weddings, births, coming out)
   I5 one-off shocks (blackouts, outages, terrorist attacks, disasters, eclipses, mass shootings)
   I6 tech and product launches (smartphones, apps, game launches, platforms such as Uber, Craigslist, Facebook)
   I7 dated clock and calendar rule changes (daylight saving time transitions or extensions, time-zone or date-line switches)
   I8 viral moments, memes, challenges (including hashtag movements such as MeToo)
   Datable means the paper gives a date or dated instances (a recurring event counts when its instances are dated: each
   Super Bowl, each spring DST transition, each release weekend). Generic exposure is not a stone (screen time, hours of
   TV, social media use in general, "media coverage", sports participation, internet use in general).
   NOT stones here: laws, policies, programs, regulations, court rulings, government interventions; the COVID-19
   pandemic and lockdowns; weather, temperature, pollution, seasons; religious observances and holidays (Ramadan,
   Christmas); ordinary calendar features (weekends, birthdays, Friday the 13th, moon phases); wars and conflicts;
   economic crises and recessions; epidemics.
2. OUTCOME: a measured outcome in real behavior or records (births, marriages, crimes, sentences, admissions, deaths,
   crashes, test scores, attendance, names, productivity, spending, donations, and so on). Attitude, opinion, knowledge or
   intention surveys alone do not count. Search interest or media attention alone does not count.
3. DESIGN: a quasi-experimental or experimental comparison against a baseline: natural experiment, difference in
   differences, regression discontinuity, event study, interrupted time series, synthetic control, instrumental variable
   based on the event, comparison with expected counts from other years or matched control days or areas, randomized
   trial. A plain before-and-after count with no trend or control does not count. Reviews and meta-analyses do not count
   unless they report a pooled effect of one named stone.

Make one row per distinct (stone, outcome). A paper may give several rows (different outcomes, or different stones).
Report a null finding as a row with direction "none".

Fields per row (JSON object, one per line):
  "paper_id": the paper's id exactly as given
  "stone": short specific name of the event as the paper frames it, e.g. "Upset losses by NFL home teams",
           "The August 2003 Northeast blackout", "Spring daylight saving time transition (US)", "Release of Grand Theft Auto IV"
  "stone_date": the date or dated instances as the paper gives them (US style: MM/DD/YYYY, MM/YYYY, YYYY, or a range such as
           "each Super Bowl, 1974-2009"); "" if the abstract gives none
  "event_class": one of I1..I8
  "outcome": one plain line, direction included, e.g. "More domestic violence calls to police on the day"
  "population_place": who and where, e.g. "US counties", "Los Angeles"
  "outcome_domain": one of
       O1 births and fertility (incl. birth weight)       O2 marriage, divorce, family
       O3 crime and violence (incl. domestic, hate crime)  O4 courts, judges, sentencing, juries
       O5 hospital admissions, injuries, mortality         O6 traffic and transport accidents
       O7 school attendance, test scores, education choices O8 names and language
       O9 work, productivity, absenteeism                   O10 spending or sales
       O11 civic acts (blood or organ donation, volunteering, census response, turnout)
       O0 none of these (write the area in "other_domain", e.g. "sleep", "stock returns", "physical activity", "mental health")
  "other_domain": "" unless O0
  "direction": "up", "down", "none" or "mixed"
  "effect": the size as the abstract states it, short ("+8% calls", "OR 1.25"); "" if not stated
  "design": the design as the abstract states it
  "quote": the paper's own words from the title or abstract that state the finding, copied exactly, under 25 words
  "excluded": null, or one of
       "suicide_selfharm_overdose"  the outcome is suicide, self-harm, suicidal behavior, or drug overdose or poisoning
       "party_vote"                 the outcome is votes or vote shares for a party or candidate
       "topic_match"                the outcome is the stone's own subject, stated aim or product: illness disclosure to
                                    screening or care for that illness; a place on screen to tourism there; a product, game,
                                    film, show or app to its own sales, viewing or use; a sport event to participation in that
                                    sport; a campaign, challenge or documentary to its stated cause; media arrival to viewing or
                                    use of that medium; the people killed or injured by a disaster or attack itself
  "exclusion_note": one line when excluded, else ""
  "law_policy": true if the outcome is a law, policy, vote or turnout, court, judge or jury decision, or government program
                uptake; else false

Also give one screen line per paper (JSON object), whether or not it yields rows:
  {"screen": true, "paper_id": "...", "qualifies": true or false, "reason": one of "ok", "no_stone", "stone_not_in_classes",
   "no_measured_outcome", "no_design", "review_or_commentary", "survey_only", "not_enough_text", "other",
   "note": short note}

When unsure whether a stone is specific and datable, or whether the design beats a baseline, mark the paper as not
qualifying and say why in the note. Do not invent effect sizes, dates or quotes.
