types_v1 (fixed rubric for S_B, relationship-type rarity)

Assign every catalyst exactly one catalyst type from its name and date only, and every candidate exactly one outcome
type from its outcome text only (not its evidence, not its catalyst). Pick the first type in the list that fits.
A myth check (a busted or disputed row) is typed by the outcome the popular story claimed.

Catalyst types
  K1 human_disaster     industrial, technological or infrastructure accidents, spills, contamination, building fires,
                        nuclear accidents, structural collapses, blackouts, product tampering
  K2 natural_disaster   earthquakes, storms, wildfires, droughts and dust storms, pandemics
  K3 violent_attack     mass shootings, bombings, terror attacks, poisonings meant to kill
  K4 screen_fiction     fictional film, TV series or episode, TV or radio drama, telenovela, stage musical
  K5 nonfiction_media   documentary, news or current-affairs program, talk or reality show, a broadcast segment, an
                        advertising slot, the arrival or spread of a medium (TV, cable, radio) or of a media export
  K6 book_print         a book, novel, nonfiction exposé or published op-ed
  K7 game_toy_product   a video game, toy, app, consumer product or fitness brand
  K8 public_figure      a public figure's illness disclosure, health act, death or suicide
  K9 sports_event       a game, tournament, result, a team's run, hosting a sporting event, an athlete's move or a
                        celebrity's presence at games
  K10 science_tech      an invention, a scientific discovery, a technology or a launch
  K11 legal_business    a court case or ruling, a law or regulation as the stone, a corporate scandal, a financial
                        crisis, a company's business decision
  K12 movement_culture  a social movement, a festival, a viral campaign

Outcome types
  O1 law                an enacted statute, act, constitutional amendment or bill signed into law
  O2 rule_order         an agency rule, regulation, standard, executive or emergency order, decree, ordinance
  O3 official_ban       a government, agency or institution prohibits or bars something
  O4 court_legal        a court ruling, lawsuit outcome, prosecution, conviction or legal action
  O5 inquiry            an investigating commission, inquiry or official review
  O6 institution        a new agency, office, organization, foundation, charity, fund, company or program body
  O7 treaty_accord      an international treaty, convention or multiparty accord
  O8 health             screening, testing, treatment, self-harm, suicide, mortality, morbidity, physical activity,
                        smoking, health care use
  O9 market             sales, purchases, demand, prices, an industry's or a company's commercial shift, a product
  O10 travel            tourism and visitor numbers
  O11 family            births, fertility, marriage, divorce, family planning, baby names
  O12 crime_justice     crime, violence, abuse, policing, sentencing patterns
  O13 civic_political   votes, elections, party membership, political attention, giving and volunteering,
                        denunciations, local government budgets
  O14 education_career  school performance, enrollment, applications, careers and recruitment
  O15 culture_naming    something named after the catalyst, a fashion or style revival, a cultural milestone
  O16 science_tech      a technology, infrastructure or scientific capability

S_B = -log2 P(outcome type | catalyst type), with P estimated over the whole evaluation pool (controls excluded) as
(n(c, o) + k * P(o)) / (n(c) + k), P(o) = (n(o) + 1) / (N + 16), k = 5 (set in config.json).
