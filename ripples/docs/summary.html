Ripple Map

A public explorer that traces where today's upstream shocks show up downstream (travel, grid demand, downloads, jobless claims, prediction markets, institutions), with a statistical test, a placebo control and a denominator on every hop.

1 · What it is and the thesis

Each day the Ripple Map picks the day's upstream shocks (a hurricane, a heat wave, a model release, a macro release, a policy decision) and draws each as a line. A stop is a downstream series in another domain that moved against its own weekday and seasonal normal, after the shock, by more than placebo pairings explain. Lines grow for weeks as slower channels report. It is an interactive explorer: no scores or streaks, and not an investment tool.

Thesis: uncover hidden downstream impacts of upstream events, with statistical attribution.

Hidden impact lives in outcome series, not attention. v5's Wikipedia hops were sound but obvious: on 2026-09-23, 82 of 716 real candidates passed against 5 of 292 decoys, almost all celebrity co-reads. v6 targets outcomes (travel, grid, claims, downloads, markets, filings); attention-only hops are capped at Likely.

A link is only worth showing with its denominator. In v1, 0 of 120 "discoveries" survived a placebo test. Every line prints paths tested, paths moved, expected flukes, and a decoy "control ripple" run through identical code. The copy says "consistent with", never "caused".

2 · Why now

  • Outcome data is free, official and keyed. FRED, BLS, EIA, Census and NOAA keys are in Vault: hourly grid demand for ~65 regions since 2015, daily air travellers since 2019, weekly state claims.
  • A second estimator exists, and the plumbing is built. BigQuery's preview AI.CAUSAL_EFFECT gives independent counterfactuals; collectors, ledger and publishing carry over from v4 and v5.
  • Nobody publishes the null. Second-order commentary is plentiful and narrative; a dated, denominated record of what actually showed up does not exist.

5 · Honest limits

  • Consistent with, not caused. No effect shares, dollar or job amounts, chain probabilities, or claims outside the (mostly US) measured geography.
  • Slow channels are slow. Jobs and institutions take 28–90 days; the cold-start hero is a labelled reconstructed line.
  • Warm-up. News, TV, Bluesky, charts and Kalshi had 0–2 days of history on 2026-09-25; they cannot support Measured until κ ≥ 0.5.
  • Equities are derived-only ({{TBD: equities collector enabled or disabled at launch}}); input-output links failed as evidence (t = −1.52).

8 · Architecture

Postgres is the engine; everything the public sees is a projection. Collectors write daily aggregates. The engine runs as SQL under pg_cron between 05:50 and 08:45 UTC and writes an append-only, hash-chained ledger. The publisher then freezes versioned JSON, pre-renders share cards, and regenerates static stubs on GitHub Pages. No request from a visitor ever reaches a source.

10 · The cascade engine

A typed mechanism graph decides what to test. An event study with three placebo families decides whether the downstream series moved. Replication across past events of the same family decides how much to trust a class of link. Candidates are frozen and hashed before any post-onset number is read, which closes the forking path that produced v1's 0 of 120.

Objects

  • Series and channels. Every series belongs to a channel, and every channel is either attention (reading, search, social, news, TV) or outcome (prediction markets, builders, consumption, physical world, jobs, institutions, economy). Each channel has a fixed statistic and clock, and the statistic is never chosen per hop.
  • Shocks. The onset is the first day an attention run crosses its threshold. Scheduled events (FOMC, CPI, jobs report, forecast landfalls, announced releases) are registered before they happen. Each shock gets a family: hazard, tech, policy, media, person or other.
  • Decoys. Eight a day, matched to real shocks on family, baseline decile and date, and calm over the previous 14 days. They go through the same generation, tests, looks and chaining. Every tier a decoy earns is false by construction.
  • Candidate paths. Family mappers (for example storm → state → air travel, grid demand, claims, warnings), the mechanism library, Wikidata neighbours, capped Wikipedia outlinks (attention-only) and co-mentions, with at most 60 per event. Input-output links generate no tests.

Baselines

Each series is compared only with itself. Counts are logged and platform totals become shares. Weekday effects are shrunk toward the source's pooled effect, and holidays are removed. A year-ago term is fitted where there are at least 400 days of history; for travel, grid and transit it is the main seasonal control, restricted to the same weekday.

ARt = (x̃t − ŷt) / σ − ct  ·  baseline B = [tp − 111, tp − 21]

σ is a robust scale (1.4826·MAD, with floors for low counts). ŷ adds a Theil–Sen growth term only when growth over 60 days exceeds σ, so steady adoption growth does not read as impact. The common-shock term ct subtracts the day's median z across a source's panel. Regional series are demeaned against their national aggregate. The per-channel statistic is a window peak (attention, markets, daily economics), a cumulative abnormal response scaled for autocorrelation (builders, consumption, physical), or the z of the first release covering the window (jobs, monthly institutions and prices).

A hop's p is the maximum over families with at least 30 draws, and a hop with fewer than two families available is capped at Likely. Besag–Clifford sequential stopping ends a family after 10 exceedances. The top 100 hops by score extend the date family to 2,000 draws, which lowers their floor to 0.0005.

Multiple testing and tiers

One weighted Benjamini–Hochberg run per day covers real and decoy hops together. Weights come from path priors (clamped to 0.2–5 and renormalised) and are frozen in the ledger at 07:34, before any data is read. Priors are fitted only on events before a fixed cutoff (2026-01-01). Replication statistics use only events after it, and positive controls are excluded from both.

Two fluke numbers are shown, each with its own label. The placebo p reads "a random pairing looks this strong about 1 in N times". The decoy-calibrated fluke rate f is decoy passes ÷ real passes, binned by strength and hiddenness over 90 days, and reads "links like this turn out to be flukes about 1 in K times". Effect sizes are shrunk for the winner's curse and shown with an 80% interval; the raw peak appears only in the math drawer.

Chaining

  • Only Likely-or-better hops get children, frozen the next morning. Order must hold (tu ≤ tv ≤ tw); a violation marks common_cause and cuts the chain.
  • Fork test. The engine also tests u → w directly. If the direct path arrives no later or is at least 80% as strong, w is drawn as a sibling, not a grandchild.
  • Mediation. w is drawn as v's child only with a reader-flow edge, a positive partial lag after removing u, or a mechanism edge that u → w lacks.
  • Route verification. With 8 or more past family events, a permutation test (10k shuffles) marks a route solid or dashed.
  • Nothing is multiplied along a chain for display. The line shows its weakest-link tier and "expected false links along this line: Σf". The public depth is at most 3, and depth 3 is collapsed under "if the previous step holds".

Evolution and retraction

Each channel has pre-registered looks: daily for attention, +3/+7 for physical, +7/+14 for builders and consumption, and each release to +28 or +90 for jobs, institutions and monthly prices. Measured is granted only at the final look. Tiers change with hysteresis: promote at qw ≤ 0.05, demote above 0.10. A published hop is retracted when data revisions or recalibration push it past 0.10, when a later common-shock or holiday flag covers its onset, when a rival event or the fork test wins, when hourly data reverses the order, or when the circuit breaker trips. After a hop is published, its attention evidence is frozen, so our own publicity cannot upgrade it.

Second opinion: BigQuery AI.CAUSAL_EFFECT

What it is. A preview function that fits a univariate ARIMA_PLUS counterfactual for one intervention timestamp. It returns the absolute and relative effect with pointwise bounds and a p-value, and runs many series in parallel through id_cols. What it is not. It does no discovery, has no controls for common shocks, no multiple-testing control and no mechanism paths, so it cannot replace the engine.

  • How it is used. A nightly GitHub Action authenticates keylessly through Workload Identity Federation. It exports Likely and Measured series to dataset ripples_ce, runs the function, and stores the relative effect, interval and p-value in Supabase.
  • Measured requires agreement. The effect sign must match, and the interval must exclude 0 at the pre-registered horizon.
  • Calibrated before it counts. It is run first on placebo events and the positive and negative controls, and its decoy false-positive rate is published: {{TBD: CAUSAL_EFFECT decoy false-positive rate}}. 11 · Sourcing rules and demarcation

Creative means more independent, legitimate signals, not grey access. Each source and path is graded by a written decision test (official channel? any barrier? robots.txt? terms? personal data? published rate limit?), and the answers are recorded in the ledger.

Hard rules

  • Honest identity. A declared User-Agent with a contact URL; no browser UA, no spoofed headers, no cookies.
  • robots.txt is honoured for every unkeyed path. A robots.txt that cannot be reached counts as a deny.
  • Stop, don't retry. A 429 or 503 stops the host until the next UTC midnight and halves the next day's cap. A 401 or 403, or a redirect to a login or challenge page, stops it until the owner clears it.
  • No circumvention: no CAPTCHA or challenge solving, no proxies or IP rotation, no key or account rotation, no logged-in scraping.
  • Aggregates only. Counts, ranks and our own z-scores; no post text, handles or article bodies.
  • Documented exceptions. Wikimedia's Action API (the operator's bot policy invites it) and SEC EDGAR full-text search (a published fair-access API, capped at 2 req/s and 200/day) skip the robots.txt check. Every other rule still applies to them.

Audit, 2026-09-25: PASS after three fixes. All outbound requests from the attention layer pass through one polite-fetch path. It applies the RED denylist, source and host checks, the kill switch, host leases, robots.txt, budgets, the Wikimedia quiet window and the contact gate. The three violations were in the older pipeline and one cron schedule: robots.txt was unchecked on the Trends RSS and Bluesky trend calls, failed jobs were requeued after a 403 or 429, and one Wikidata job ran inside the quiet window. All three are fixed. Final re-audit after last deploys: {{TBD: re-run RED grep and per-host audit on final build}}.

13 · Honesty guardrails

We can claim

  • Timing: the stop moved after the shock, with the lag
  • Size against the series' own weekday and seasonal normal, shrunk, with an interval
  • Rarity against calibrated placebos
  • How many independent channels agree
  • A named mechanism, labelled by its source
  • Replication across past events of the same family
  • The day's and the line's denominators

We never claim

  • "Caused", "drove", "because of", "flooded into"
  • A share of an effect, dollar or job amounts, individual behaviour
  • Any probability for a whole chain
  • Input-output links as evidence
  • Tickers, trades or price forecasts
  • "No effect" beyond the published power curve
  • A headline pointing at a Watching or Likely stop
  • Numbers come only from SQL. AI-written text, if the daily Routine is ever approved, is limited to labelled mechanism captions and never adds a hop, number or tier.
  • The failures stay on screen. Stubs for stops that stayed flat, lines that went nowhere, the control ripple, retractions, failed gates and the v1 post-mortem.
  • Published cards never change. Shared links point to frozen versions, and a note says how much the line has grown since.
  • Quiet mode for harm. Hazards with casualties get neutral copy, no animation and no teaser card; the data is still shown.
  • Reconstructed lines are labelled on every surface, and they are excluded from live denominators.
