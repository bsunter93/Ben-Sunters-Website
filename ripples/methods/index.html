Ripple Map: data collection & contact

Ripple Map is a research project by Ben Sunter that measures how events spill over into public attention and everyday activity. This page is the contact named in its crawler's User-Agent.

Who is making requests

Requests identify themselves as ripples-research/0.2 (+https://bensunter.com/ripples/methods/). They come from scheduled jobs on Supabase and GitHub Actions.

What it collects

  • Only public, aggregate counts: daily pageviews, public statistics series, and item counts from documented APIs.
  • No personal data, no account data, no content behind a login.

How it behaves

  • Documented APIs are used where they exist, and robots.txt is respected everywhere else.
  • Requests are one at a time and well under each provider's published limits. For Wikimedia that is at most one request per second.
  • On any HTTP 429 or 503 it stops, and it does not retry until the next scheduled run.
  • It never uses proxies, rotating IPs, a disguised User-Agent, or ways around CAPTCHAs or bot checks.

Contact / opt-out

If this crawler is causing problems, or you'd like a source excluded, email sunterbb@gmail.com. Requests are honoured promptly, and each source has a kill switch.
