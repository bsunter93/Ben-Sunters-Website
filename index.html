About

From Strategy to Execution

I'm an operations leader who embeds within critical cross-functional initiatives to ensure strategic clarity and drive outcomes. My Industrial and Systems Engineering background from the University of Florida trained me to see systems, identify bottlenecks, and build frameworks that scale.

What sets me apart: I'm technical. I build the dashboards and automated reporting that surface insights to leadership. I write SQL to extract data, Python to automate workflows, and design executive-ready visualizations in Tableau and Power BI. Strategy is only as good as the data behind it.

"I serve as a control tower during periods of significant change—mapping impacts, driving alignment, and keeping stakeholders informed, accountable, and focused on results."
