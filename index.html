01How I think

Five steps, in order

The same sequence on a merger, a cost program and an annual planning cycle.

  1. Frame

    Name the decision actually being made, and who owns it.

  2. Model

    Turn the ambiguity into constraints, resources, dependencies and dates.

  3. Diagnose

    Find the binding constraint, and what it costs while it holds.

  4. Intervene

    Compare the options on impact, on cost, and on when the effect lands.

  5. Operationalize

    Hand it over as ownership, cadence, and a number that keeps arriving.
